# Engineering Principles

## Simplicity wins

Choose the simplest solution that solves today's problem.

---

## Prefer composition over complexity

Small modules.

Small functions.

Small classes.

---

## Data is more important than AI

If data quality improves, AI improves.

If AI improves but data is wrong, recommendations are wrong.

---

## Optimise for readability

Future Tony is the most important developer.

---

## Automate repetitive work

If you do something twice,
consider scripting it.

---

## Measure before optimising

Never optimise code because it feels slow.

Measure first.

---

## Every feature should be testable

If it can't be tested,
it's probably too tightly coupled.
