# Chapter 14 — ChatGPT

**Badges:** 📖 🧪 💻

---

## Purpose

ChatGPT acts as the project's **technical mentor** — product owner, architecture,
planning and documentation guidance.

---

## Why We Use It

Defined in the `CLAUDE.md` AI Team Roles. ChatGPT helps shape *what* to build and
*why* (vision, roadmap, sprint framing), while Claude Code handles *how* it's built.
Together they mirror a product-owner / developer split.

---

## Concepts

- **Mentor, not autopilot:** guidance and options, with Tony deciding.
- **Planning role:** roadmap, sprint shaping, breaking work into user stories.
- **Documentation role:** helping structure the docs (this handbook was ChatGPT's
  suggestion).

---

## Examples

The overall documentation structure — Charter, Roadmap, Sprint plans, and the idea
for this Developer Handbook (including the badge system) — came from ChatGPT's
mentoring.

---

## Best Practices

- Use it to explore options and understand trade-offs before committing.
- Bring its suggestions back into the repo as real docs/decisions (e.g. ADRs), so
  the reasoning is recorded, not lost in a chat.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Treating suggestions as final without checking them against the project's own
  principles and constraints.

---

## Lessons Learned

- Separating "what/why" (ChatGPT) from "how" (Claude Code) keeps each conversation
  focused.
