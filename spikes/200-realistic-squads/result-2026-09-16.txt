The population ADR-199 gated two thresholds on, and the two thresholds it unblocked.

WHY RANDOM SQUADS WERE THE WRONG POPULATION - the number that settles it:

    random legal squad   121.3 XI xP over 5 GWs
    optimiser's own 15   291.1
    template (most-owned within budget)  226.6
    the owner's TS       231.1
    the owner's RoboTS   244.2

A random squad is not a bad squad, it is barely a squad. Everything looks improvable against it.

THE QUALITY LADDER (optimal, perturbed by k legal swaps)
    k=0  291.1     k=4  274.2     k=9   227.0
    k=2  283.5     k=6  263.3     k=12  208.1

...AND WHY QUALITY ALONE WAS NOT ENOUGH:

    k=9       XI 227.0   best transfer p75  2.88
    template  XI 226.6   best transfer p75  1.60

Same quality, very different improvability. Perturbing the optimum creates REPAIRABLE HOLES - a few
specific bad players, each individually replaceable. A template squad is UNIFORMLY MEDIOCRE: no single
glaring weakness to fix. Real squads behave like the template, not like a perturbed optimum.

*** The population had to be shaped like a real squad, not merely scored like one. ***
