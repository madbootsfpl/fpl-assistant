--- what it says ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The SECOND TRANSFER is the big missing number, not the better single one. It is worth roughly as
   much again as the first (+18.4 mean; +4.5 and +10.0 on the two real squads), and the app never
   shows it — `week_plan` computes a 2nd move and uses it ONLY to answer "bank or use", never to
   recommend.
2. Planning the pair jointly is usually small (+0.4 mean, 8/30 squads) but occasionally large:
   RoboTS +5.9, because greedy routes Ballard in through the WRONG sale (Hume) and that blocks the
   pair that wanted Ballard via Mukiele so the money could also fund Watkins → Isak.
   ⚠️ n=2 on real squads. This gap needs a proper measurement on realistic squads before it justifies
   a joint search — random squads systematically understate it (they have so much headroom that any
   two moves gain a lot, so the ORDER matters less).
3. The app does not know how many free transfers you hold, except on the Transfer tab's own
   number_input, which feeds "bank or use" and nothing else.
