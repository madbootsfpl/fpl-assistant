--- THE HYPOTHESIS IS REFUTED -----------------------------------------------------------------------
Restricted to the players it applies to, the term does not look better. It looks WORSE:

    whole board   ρ 0.619 → 0.605   (−0.014 across the sweep)
    DEF/GK only   ρ 0.546 → 0.520   (−0.026 — nearly twice the decline, on half the sample)
    MAE           1.33 → 1.38 on DEF/GK, against 1.22 → 1.24 whole-board

That is the double-count reading of ADR-188 confirmed from a second direction. A defender's own
points-per-90 ALREADY contains the clean sheets he kept, so adding his club's rate on top re-states
information the model has — and it does the most damage exactly where it is applied, by over-rating
defenders at good clubs relative to their own record.

--- THE ONE THING THAT MOVES THE OTHER WAY, AND WHY IT IS NOT EVIDENCE -------------------------------
hit@10 among DEF/GK rises 0.15 → 0.23 at weights 0.15–0.30, i.e. the TOP of the defender board improves
while the overall ordering gets worse. That is a tempting result: a transfer recommendation cares about
the top of the board, not the rank correlation of 277 defenders most of whom nobody owns.

It is 3 players. hit@10 averaged over 4 gameweeks means 6.0 → 9.2 correct picks out of 40. At n=4
gameweeks that is noise, it is a SECONDARY criterion under §B0, and the PRIMARY one (ρ) declines on the
same rows. Reading it as signal would be choosing the metric after seeing the curve — the exact thing
§B0's pre-registration exists to stop.

Recorded because it is the honest shape of the result, and because if it survives at GW6 with more
gameweeks behind it, it is worth a proper look at whether a top-of-board metric belongs in §B0 at all.

--- CONCLUSION --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CLEAN_SHEET_WEIGHT stays 0, now for a stronger reason than "the curve declined": it declines on the
population it was built for. The GW6 sitting still owns the decision — this is 4 gameweeks.

Note: the whole-board baseline reads 0.619 here against 0.621 in ADR-188 §📉. Same 4 rounds; the player
table has refreshed since. The difference is a third of one SE and changes nothing.
