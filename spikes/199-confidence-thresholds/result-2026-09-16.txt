--- SET: _CLEAR_LEAD 0.8 -> 1.3 -----------------------------------------------------------------------
Needs NO new measurement. _CLEAR_LEAD and the captain's CLEAR are the SAME QUANTITY on the SAME
DISTRIBUTION - how clear a captain lead is. One was measured over 300 squads and re-measured at GW4; the
other was typed. They disagree, and the typed one is the one inside the confidence score.

--- SET: _CLEAR_CHIP_MARGIN 0.15 -> 0.04 ---------------------------------------------------------------
Chip margins are a property of the FIXTURE LIST, not of squad quality, so the random-squad population is
valid here. p75 is 0.04; 0.15 is ~4x that, so "clear" is effectively unreachable.

--- GATED: _CLEAR_GAIN and _CLEAR_REBUILD --------------------------------------------------------------
WRONG POPULATION, and spike 191 already found why: random squads have far more headroom than real ones.
The owner's actual squad's best move was +1.8 against a random squad's +3.5; his real wildcard read 0.40
of his own projection against a random squad's 1.30.

Setting either from this distribution would repeat the clean-sheet-rate mistake exactly: a confident
number from an instrument pointed at the wrong thing. They need a realistic-squad population first
(optimiser-built or imported), which is its own measurement.

--- GATED: FLAG_COST 8 ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not a margin, so the p75 rule does not apply. The honest question is different: how much does a flagged
player actually cost a gameweek's return? That is a backtest against real outcomes, not a distribution of
margins, and it belongs with the §B0 machinery rather than here.
